About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple tool that helps you capture and organize important ideas, reminders, and tasks. It is designed to make note-taking quick and easy so you can keep track of what matters.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes and go to the note section.
  2. Type your idea, reminder, or task in the note field.
  3. Click the Add Note button to save your note.
  4. Review your notes whenever you need them.

Features

  • Quick and simple note-taking.
  • Easy-to-use interface.
  • Organized list of notes.
  • Keyboard shortcuts for common actions.
  • Simple and accessible design.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Create a new note
Ctrl + S Save your note
